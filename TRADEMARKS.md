# Trademark Guidelines for DreamNet Spore SDK

The Apache 2.0 license grants software rights, but **does not grant trademark rights**.

### 1. Allowed Fair Use
- You may state that your application is "Built with DreamNet Spore SDK" or "Compatible with DreamNet Spore Node".
- You may use the un-modified open-source code for commercial or non-commercial deployments.

### 2. Restricted Usage
- You may not represent a modified fork as the "Official DreamNet Spore Node".
- You may not use "DreamNet" in your company, domain name, or primary branding without written consent.
